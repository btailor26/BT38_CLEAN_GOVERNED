Operational attention

One system. Multiple marketplace operations.

A central management layer for multi-channel selling.

Running a multi-channel business means managing stock, orders, fulfilment, shipping and marketplace connections across different platforms.

BT38 brings those operational workflows together so sellers have a clearer view of what is happening and what requires attention.

Instead of treating each marketplace as an isolated operation, BT38 provides a central management layer around your warehouse stock, connected stores and fulfilment workflows.

Controlled marketplace management

Built around controlled operational workflows.

Marketplace connections, stock movements and operational actions are handled through defined management workflows, with safeguards around synchronisation and marketplace updates.

Sellers remain in control of their marketplace accounts while BT38 provides the management tools needed to coordinate day-to-day operations.
